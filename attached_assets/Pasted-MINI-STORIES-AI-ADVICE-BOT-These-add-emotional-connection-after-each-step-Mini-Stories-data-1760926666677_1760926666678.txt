1️⃣ Inputs → Inferences Map

Income + dependents	Recommended savings %	base 20% ± dependents (−3% each)

Risk answers	Risk Score (1–5)	Sleep fine → 5, Panic → 1
Risk Score	Allocation model	{E:D:G} = {60:30:10}, {40:50:10}, etc.
2️⃣ Corpus Calculation
Target Corpus = (Expected Monthly Expense × 12) × ((1 - (1 + real_return)^-retirement_years) / real_return)


Real return = Nominal return – Inflation

Inflation = auto from RBI trend (6%) or city index

Real return assumption = 3%–6% based on risk level

3️⃣ Freedom Score (0–100)
Freedom Score = (Current Corpus + FV of savings) / Target Corpus × 100


100 = perfectly on track

70–99 = needs moderate savings bump

<70 = underprepared → trigger advice bot
