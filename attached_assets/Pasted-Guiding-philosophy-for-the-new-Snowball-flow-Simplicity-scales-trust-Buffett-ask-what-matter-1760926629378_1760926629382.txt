Guiding philosophy for the new Snowball flow

“Simplicity scales trust” — (Buffett): ask what matters, ignore what doesn’t.

“Clarity > Complexity” — users don’t want a finance exam; they want to feel understood.

“Lifestyle defines corpus, not numbers alone.”

“Your time horizon is your biggest edge.” (Buffett/Damani both obsess over compounding time, not market timing).

Let the AI do the math. Let the user do the dreaming.

🧭 The new 5-Step Snowball Flow (7 → 5, but richer under the hood)

You’ll see it’s simpler for the user but more intelligent behind the scenes.
Each step feels conversational but unlocks deep inferences.

Step 1 – Your Life Snapshot

Purpose: capture age, income, and dependents without feeling like a form.

Questions (only 3):

“Hey {name}, how old are you and when do you wish to retire?”

“What’s your current monthly take-home income?”

“Do you have anyone financially depending on you right now?” (Yes / No → if yes: how many)

AI inference:

Infers working years left.

Uses demographic & average spending ratios to estimate baseline expenses (if not provided).

Adjusts savings-rate recommendations for dependents.

💡 Inspired by Buffett’s “Don’t risk what you have for what you don’t need.”
Dependents tighten safety margins; Snowball auto-adds safety buffer for them.

Step 2 – Your Money Flow

Purpose: understand saving and spending without granular breakup.

Questions (only 2):

“How much of your income do you typically manage to save or invest each month?” (slider 0–50%)

“Roughly, how much do you spend on essentials vs lifestyle?” (auto-split if unsure → defaults to 60 : 40)

AI inference:

Projects inflation-adjusted expense growth.

Calculates real savings rate.

Builds early corpus estimate through rule of 72 style growth.

🧮 Applies Damani’s philosophy: discipline > timing.
He famously said you build wealth “not by heroics, but by habits.”

Step 3 – Your Assets & Obligations

Purpose: know where the money already is, and what drags it down.

Questions (only 2):

“How much do you already have invested (including savings, stocks, mutual funds, PF, etc.)?”

“Any ongoing big loans?” (Yes / No → if yes, only EMI + years left)

AI inference:

Categorizes corpus into liquid vs illiquid assets using heuristics (based on amount and age).

Uses Buffett’s asset-allocation rule of thumb (“Never invest in something you don’t understand”) → conservative if too little liquidity.

Deducts EMI-based drain from monthly savings.

Step 4 – Your Dream Retirement

Purpose: capture lifestyle intent and longevity in the simplest way.

Questions (only 3):

“What kind of retirement do you imagine?”

Modest / Comfortable / Luxury / Nomadic (choices mapped to 70% / 90% / 120% / 110% income replacement ratio).

“Where do you plan to live after retiring?” (AI fetches cost-of-living index differential).

“How long do you want to plan your money to last?” (default = life expectancy table + option to adjust).

AI inference:

Uses lifestyle choice + city COLI + inflation forecast → post-retirement monthly need.

Applies SEBI & HDFC guidelines for replacement ratio.

Runs corpus requirement simulation automatically.

🧘 Morgan Housel influence: people don’t want millions; they want “independence.”
So Snowball reframes this step as “How free do you want to be?” not “How rich?”

Step 5 – Your Risk & Route

Purpose: auto-determine suitable strategy with minimum input.

Questions (only 2):

“How would you feel if your portfolio dropped 20% next year?” → “Sleep fine / Worried / Panic”

“Do you prefer steady income or growth that fluctuates?” → slider Income ↔ Growth

AI inference:

Calculates risk score (1-5).

Builds asset-allocation glidepath:

High risk → 60 E / 30 D / 10 G

Medium → 50 / 40 / 10

Low → 30 / 60 / 10

Computes target corpus with real-return assumptions per risk class.

⚖️ Buffett mindset: “Risk comes from not knowing what you’re doing.”
So Snowball doesn’t ask for numbers — it infers behavior.

✨ Optional Step 6 – Reality Check (Passive Insights)

No questions — Snowball just shows:

Corpus needed vs on-track amount

Years to goal

Suggested monthly increase or asset rebalance

A “Retirement Freedom Score” out of 100

(Users love this shareable visual — similar to Nudge’s “Wealth Wellness Score.”)

🧠 Behind the scenes (AI layer doing the heavy lifting)

AI Income–Expense Model: uses Indian household data to estimate expenses from income if not entered.

Lifestyle Mapping Engine: translates “modest/comfortable/luxury” into income replacement ratios.

Corpus Calculator: uses inflation-adjusted PV of annuity formula.

Behavior Inference: risk score = weighted reaction × savings habit × dependents.

Rule Engine based on legends:

Buffett’s 20-slot rule → don’t over-diversify.

Damani’s “cash is a position” → suggests cash allocation if user is overexposed.

Charlie Munger’s mental model → bias penalty if user selects “Panic” response.
