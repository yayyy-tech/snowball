Snowball Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 22, 2025
Snowball is a retirement-planning platform that helps users model, track and optimise their long-term financial plans using AI. Your privacy matters to us. This policy explains what we collect, why we collect it, how we use it, and the choices you have.

By using Snowball, you agree to the practices described here.

1. What We Collect

We only collect information that is needed to provide and improve Snowball.

a. Retirement Planning Data

Inputs you share while creating or updating retirement plans, such as:

savings amount

expected retirement age

expected returns

financial assumptions

edits or versions of your past plans

b. Usage Data

Information about how you use the product, like:

actions taken inside the app

timestamps for plan creation/edits

general feature-usage patterns

c. Technical Data

Standard technical information such as:

device type

browser type

IP address

cookies that help us run the platform

What We Don’t Collect

We do not collect or store personal identity information like your address, Aadhaar, PAN, phone number, or anything that can directly identify you unless you provide it voluntarily for future features.

2. Why We Collect It

We use the data for the following reasons:

To run Snowball
Your plan inputs are needed to generate retirement projections and insights.

To help you revisit past plans
We save your historical plans so you can compare or track changes.

To improve our AI models
We use anonymised plan data to refine GPT-based suggestions and improve accuracy.

To improve product performance
Usage patterns help us understand what works and what needs improvement.

To keep the experience secure
Technical logs help detect bugs, fraud, or misuse.

3. How We Process Your Data

We only process data after obtaining clear consent from you.

You can withdraw consent anytime from settings or by contacting us.

Processing is limited to the specific purpose you agreed to.

All data used for AI optimisation is anonymised or pseudonymised.

4. How We Protect Your Data

We use industry-standard security measures, including:

encryption in transit and at rest

access-control systems

monitoring and audit logs

routine security reviews

Only authorised systems or personnel can access your data.

5. How Long We Keep Your Data

Retirement-plan data stays with us as long as your account is active or until you delete it.

Usage and technical logs may be retained for a limited period for security, analytics or compliance.

When you delete your account or request deletion, we remove your data unless we must keep some information for legal obligations.

6. Sharing of Data

We do not sell your data.

We may share anonymised or aggregated data with:

analytics partners

infrastructure/service providers

research or AI-training pipelines

No third party receives information that can identify you personally.

If we ever introduce integrations with external apps, we will ask for fresh consent.

7. Your Rights

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act (India), you have the right to:

Access the data we hold about you

Correct information that’s inaccurate

Delete your data or your account

Withdraw consent

Know who we have shared your data with

You can send a request to us at [insert contact email].

8. Data Transfer

If your data is processed or stored outside India, we ensure it is handled under protection standards aligned with Indian law.

9. Cookies

We use cookies for:

login/session management

analytics

improving app performance

You can disable cookies, but some features may not work properly.

10. Children’s Data

Snowball is not built for users under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from minors. If we learn that data belongs to a minor, we delete it.

11. Updates to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time.
If the changes are significant, we will notify you through the app or website.

The latest version will always be available on our site.
